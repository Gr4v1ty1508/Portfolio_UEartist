Lighting

Forgotten, a mansion at night

Forgotten is my senior project, a first-person horror game. The player only has a lantern and a camcorder, so light decides what the player sees and where they dare to go. I did the lighting pass for the house: where light comes from, its color and strength, and the final grade.

Light sources

Where light comes from in each room and how it pulls the player toward the next door.

Atmosphere

Shadow and contrast that keep far corners unreadable, so the player never feels safe.

Grade

Post-process volume for exposure, contrast and color, tuned so dark areas stay dark but still read.

What's mine: lighting and post-process for the whole house. The modular house kit, furniture and their textures were made by others on the project.
